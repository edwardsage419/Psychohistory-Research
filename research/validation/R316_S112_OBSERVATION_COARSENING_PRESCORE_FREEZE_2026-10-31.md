## Fixed source and mathematical question
Use immutable original R316 four-node fixed blocks (0,0,1,1), six undirected dyads in original order, and 64 graph masks for each of two known Bernoulli regimes A=(within 3/4,cross 1/4), D=(within 1/4,cross 3/4). Original complete graph source: research/R316/R316_EXACT_128_GRAPH_LIKELIHOODS.json (Git blob 36da587adbc6e1d92824a5fd39516e506569166c). Original dyad atom CSV and all R316 acceptance records remain unchanged.

Question: with a single exactly observed artificial graph, how much discrimination information is lost by coarsening the observation? Compare six predeclared deterministic observation maps: full labeled 6-bit graph; ordered pair (w,c) of within/cross edge counts; signed count w-c; within count w alone; cross count c alone; total edge count k=w+c. Here w ranges 0..2 and c ranges 0..4. The block assignment and regime probabilities are known, and the two regime hypotheses have equal prior probability 1/2.

For each map O, group all 64 graphs by its observed value. Compute exact rational likelihood masses P(O=o|A), P(O=o|D). Frozen optimal zero-one Bayes classification accuracy is (1/2) sum_o max(P_A(o),P_D(o)). Ties are assigned to A in a deterministic label classifier but the reported accuracy is tie-independent. Also report exact total variation TV_O=(1/2) sum_o |P_A(o)-P_D(o)| and verify accuracy=(1+TV_O)/2. Full-graph TV must equal original R316 203/256. Verify the information coarsening inequalities: full >= (w,c) >= (w-c), and full >= (w,c) >= w, c, k; compare all values without assuming strict inequality. Verify each observation distribution normalizes to one and every source mask occurs once per regime. No parameter fitting, holdout, calibration or human sample.
