## Frozen source and finite target
Use the original project-defined **synchronous discrete five-agent SIR** mechanism, not Kermack and McKendrick's continuous-time equations. N=5, 21 compartment states (S,I,R), S+I+R=5, p,q each in {0,1/2,1}, 189 worlds. Keep p,q constant over exactly two successive transitions. New infectives at step t are eligible for contacts and recovery only at step t+1. Recovered never become susceptible. Original R309 archived one-step complete source has 1,134 event rows and SHA256 9470c1e1c880f51da5c11377b6cc8be7f799542d0cebd1f82d4fc3539cabd134. Verify the reconstructed original byte identity before any use; if mismatch, STOP and report failure.

For each initial world w, compute K(w,u) by summing the original one-step joint events that reach u. The two-step transition is K²(w,v)=sum_u K(w,u)K(u,v), retaining p,q. Compute all 189 x 21=3,969 (world, final state) cells, including exact zeros, with Python Fraction. Exact outcomes: P(I_1=0), P(I_2=0), P(I_1>0,I_2=0)=P(I_2=0)-P(I_1=0), E[S_2], E[I_2], E[R_2]. Also compute P(X_2>0) by full two-stage event paths, with X_2 the second-step new infections. All 189 worlds weighted equally only for descriptive aggregate diagnostics, never treated as empirical observations.
