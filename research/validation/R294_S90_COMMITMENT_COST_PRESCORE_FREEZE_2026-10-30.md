## Predeclared construction
Retain the exact 72 original worlds, their a,b, family and k/beta/delta parameters, dates t=0 and t=1, and original tie rule (choose earlier A on exact equality). Discounting uses D_h(d)=1/(1+kd), D_e(d)=delta^d, and D_q(0)=1, D_q(d>0)=beta delta^d. No new parameter fitting.
At t=1 the uncommitted agent implements the original t=1 choice. A commitment contract at t=0, if purchased, binds the agent to its t=0 preferred option at t=1. The fee is measured directly in t=0 utility units and is paid at t=0, with no secondary discount. Fee grid C={0,1/4,1/2,1}. Purchase only if the gain in t=0 discounted option value from enforcing its t=0 choice over uncommitted implementation is strictly greater than C. On equality do not purchase. If original t=0 and t=1 choices agree, gain=0 and never purchase. If t=0=B and t=1=A, gain=V_B(t0)-V_A(t0)>0. No other reversal direction is expected; if present, compute gain from t0 preferred option minus t1 option and record it explicitly.
For each 72 x 4 = 288 cells record world ID, family, original choices, fee, exact gain, purchase, implementation, net ex ante improvement max(gain-fee,0), including zero-fee non-reversals. Aggregate by family and fee: purchase count and sum/mean net improvement. The 72 worlds are equally weighted only for a descriptive artificial-grid average; no population interpretation.
