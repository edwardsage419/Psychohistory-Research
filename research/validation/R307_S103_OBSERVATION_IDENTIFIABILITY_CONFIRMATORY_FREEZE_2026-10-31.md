## Frozen exact universe and observational contracts
Use the original fixed formula O=(p/(1-p))*(1+c*k)^t; private b=O/(1+O); public(m)=p+(1-n*(6-m)/6)*(b-p). Hold p=1/2. For each t in {1,2,3}, enumerate all 27 triples (c,k,n) in {0,1/2,1}^3, 81 confirmatory worlds total. Compare exactly six designs per t: D0 no exposure joint at m=1 (negative control, force t=0); D1 private at m=1; D2 public at m=1; D3 public at m=1 and m=2; D4 private and public at m=1; D5 private and public at both m. A signature is the ordered tuple of exact rational observation probabilities, with known p,t,m and no sampling noise. Partition 27 candidate triples within each t. Count signatures, class-size histogram, singleton candidate worlds, maximum class size, and uniform-prior optimal exact-identification probability = number of distinct signatures / 27. Explicitly test D3 vs D4 and D4 vs D5; any improvement or no improvement is reportable. No changes to formulas, grid or designs after seeing confirmatory results.
