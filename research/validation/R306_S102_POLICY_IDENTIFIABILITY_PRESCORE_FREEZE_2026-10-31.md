## Question and fixed finite universe
Can a researcher uniquely identify which of the four frozen R306 policies generated a perfectly measured post-discussion group consensus mean when the complete three-person prediscussion opinion triplet and argument direction are observed? How much is gained by observing the SAME artificial policy in both supporting and counterargument contexts? Do not infer hidden policy from mere consensus convergence.

Enumerate all 27 ordered group triplets from {-1/2,0,1/2}^3, the four original policies AVG (alpha,beta)=(0,0), SOCIAL=(1/2,0), ARG=(0,1/2), COMBINED=(1/2,1/2), and both original argument contexts SUPPORT and COUNTER. Preserve m=mean(x), q=sgn(m), c=max(x) if q>0, min(x) if q<0, else 0, a=q for SUPPORT and a=-q for COUNTER. Original output m_post=m+alpha(c-m)+beta(a-m), using Python Fraction exact arithmetic. All 216 source worlds already archived by R306; this extension reuses the finite universe without adding new human data.

## Frozen observation designs and metrics
Design SUPPORT_ONLY observes (group, m_post SUPPORT); COUNTER_ONLY observes (group, m_post COUNTER); BOTH_CONTEXTS observes (group, ordered pair of m_post SUPPORT and COUNTER), with the SAME latent policy assumed stable across contexts. All group triplets are public and their order is fixed. For each design and group, partition four policy labels by exactly equal observed signature. Record class-size multiset, number of distinct signatures, number of uniquely identifiable policy labels, number of ambiguous labels, number of groups with all four uniquely identifiable, and group-averaged Bayes-optimal policy identification accuracy with a uniform prior over the four policies and zero observation noise. Accuracy is sum(number_of_classes / 4) across 27 groups divided by 27. Ties are irreducible; no learned classifier or fitted parameters. Report all 27 group-level partitions, all 216 original source policy/context rows, and the three design summaries. No statistical confidence intervals for a complete artificial enumeration.

## Fixed controls and decision rule
All seven zero-mean group triplets must yield identical output 0 across four policies and both contexts. Two all-positive/all-negative homogeneous triplets have AVG and SOCIAL identical under either context. Exact sign-mirror invariance must hold. Both-context partitions must refine each single-context partition; two-context identification accuracy cannot be lower. At least one strict gain or an exact equality result must be reported, including all failures. Test no duplicate/missing triplets, policies or contexts; test swapped support/counter label, altered consensus, altered class size, altered accuracy, missing class, and wrong E-level via explicit exceptions that remain active under python -O.
