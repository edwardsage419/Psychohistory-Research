## New artificial controlled dynamics, distinct from authors' original empirical equations

For a strictly **constructed**, dimensionless [0,1] vector \((M_t,E_t,F_t)\) representing labels mass/elite/fiscal artificial pressure, a permanent synthetic input H, and project-defined coefficients a,b,c and r,
\[
M_{t+1}=(1-a)M_t+aH,\quad
E_{t+1}=(1-b)E_t+bM_t,\quad
F_{t+1}=(1-c)F_t+c(1-r)E_t.
\]
Every variable begins at zero. The three equations are **new project lag filters**, selected for algebraic tractability and transparent interpretation; they have no calibration to demographic, elite recruitment, government debt or observed riot rates. The multiplicative \(\Psi_t=M_tE_tF_t\) parallels the *conceptual factorization* in Turchin 2013 but **M, E, F are synthetic unit-scale toy values**, NOT the paper's observed wage, urbanization, age, elite-income, debt and trust formulas. The alternative mean \(Q_t=(M_t+E_t+F_t)/3\) is expressly a *new diagnostic only*, not Turchin's PSI. Neither index is a probability, forecast, or validated risk scale.

Freeze H∈{1/4,1/2,1}, a,b,c∈{1/4,1/2} independently, r∈{0,1/2,1}: **72 distinct worlds, each with 13 timestamps t=0,...,12**. Store full M,E,F,Psi,Q and parameter identities in [223711-byte complete JSON](R157_EXACT_RESULTS.json). Two genuinely separate exact algorithms: direct concurrent three-equation Fraction updates and independent affine matrix-power sum \(x_t=\sum_{k=0}^{t-1}A^k b\). In this expression b denotes an input vector and is distinct from the elite lag parameter b. Matrix \(A\) has rows \((1-a,0,0),(b,1-b,0),(0,c(1-r),1-c)\), driving vector \((aH,0,0)\). **All 72×13×3 = 2808 state entries agree by strict rational equality** between the algorithms. Each Psi and Q also equals its stored exact Fraction expression.

## Predeclared mathematical contrasts

**Sequential lag**: H=1, a=b=c=1/4, r=0. States after time 1,2,3:
\[
x_1=(1/4,0,0),\quad x_2=(7/16,1/16,0),\quad x_3=(37/64,5/32,1/64).
\]
Psi is zero at t=1 and t=2 even while one/two component pressures have nonzero values; Psi at t=3 is \(185/131072\). The lag and the zero-product results are mathematical properties of this construction, not historically measured political process delays.

**One-snapshot parameter ambiguity**: with common b=c=1/4,r=0, choose (H,a)=(1,1/4) and (1/2,1/2). Both generate identical time-one state \((1/4,0,0)\), whereas their time-two M values are 7/16 versus 3/8. A single snapshot does not identify input pressure and adjustment speed. Full resolved trajectories could distinguish them, so do not claim global identification failure for arbitrary observation plans.

**Index ranking reversal**: synthetic state U=(1,1,0) has arithmetic mean 2/3 and product 0. State V=(1/2,1/2,1/2) has mean 1/2 and product 1/8. Thus the mean ranks U above V, while the product ranks V above U. These deterministic constructions demonstrate dependence on *the assumed aggregation formula*. They are not evidence that either formula predicts any event.

**Fiscal-buffer counterfactual**: for fixed H,a,b,c, r=0, 1/2,1 leaves the M and E paths strictly equal; F and the product Psi decline (weakly) as r rises. When r=1, F and product Psi are identically zero even as M and E rise. It would be **invalid to interpret the zero index as a guarantee of peaceful history**. The dynamics converge asymptotically to \((H,H,(1-r)H)\), conditional on the strictly positive lag rates. No true budget identities, policy effect or institutional causal treatment is inferred.
