## Primary event-history theoretical targets

* David R. Cox (1972), *Regression Models and Life-Tables*, *Journal of the Royal Statistical Society, Series B* 34(2):187–202 (original article; subsequent published discussion occupies additional pages), DOI [10.1111/j.2517-6161.1972.tb00899.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.2517-6161.1972.tb00899.x). Original development of proportional hazards and partial likelihood. The Cox hazard conditional on surviving to t is not a cumulative probability and requires explicit risk sets/ties/censoring specification.
* Frances Stokes Berry and William D. Berry (1990), [*State Lottery Adoptions as Policy Innovations: An Event History Analysis*](https://doi.org/10.2307/1963526), *American Political Science Review* 84(2):395–415. Original publisher [Cambridge abstract](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/american-political-science-review/article/state-lottery-adoptions-as-policy-innovations-an-event-history-analysis/9B4EAF46DD866B9CEFFB6CA36861103A) distinguishes **state-internal determinants** of policy adoption from **regional exposure/diffusion** and unifies them in event-history analysis. The original lottery adoption data and county/state predictors have **not** yet been retrieved or reanalyzed. No historical lottery findings should be claimed from a synthetic toy panel.
* Srinivas C. Parinandi (2020), *Policy Inventing and Borrowing among State Legislatures*, *American Journal of Political Science* 64(4):852–868, DOI [10.1111/ajps.12513](https://doi.org/10.1111/ajps.12513). Original [Harvard Dataverse replication dataset DOI 10.7910/DVN/PMYBBM](https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/PMYBBM) includes US policy-provision adoption across states and pooled logistic event-history analysis; its repository identifies verified article figure/table replication but does not itself give this project a sample-wise held-out forecast.
* Aníbal Pérez-Liñán and Scott Mainwaring (2025), *Democratic Trajectories in the Third Wave: Aligning Theory and Methods*, *British Journal of Political Science*, original [Harvard Dataverse replication dataset DOI10.7910/DVN/WFO1N1](https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/WFO1N1). Dataset description reports 103 transitions to democracy in 84 countries, with 1,731 annual observations from first full year following transition until a breakdown or censoring at 2017; actual event and censoring coding has **not yet been inspected in raw files**. This provides a plausible historical *regime-survival* event-time study distinct from policy-adoption time; do not mix the different risk sets.

## Concrete real-data access priority

First ask the **official Harvard Dataverse API** for file-level metadata, original version, actual files, license and restriction flags of `doi:10.7910/DVN/WFO1N1`, without assuming a file is accessible solely because the catalog gives download choices. Inspect codebooks and participant/country-year rows **before** freezing a survival model or selecting event endpoints. If original data are not accessible or provenance/licensing is unclear, record the access blocker, then consider the Parinandi policy diffusion replication materials or a similarly licensed first-hand historical dataset.

A correct future historical event analysis must freeze:
1. Clearly defined event and comparison population/observation clock (new adoption, breakdown, return to democracy, or other event—never ambiguous).
2. Delayed entry/left truncation, interval/time aggregation, risk-set eligibility, repeat episodes, within-country correlation and right censoring, competing events, missingness.
3. An interpretable baseline (nonparametric Kaplan–Meier / empirical hazard), a discrete-time event-history logistic or complementary log-log model, a well-defined Cox partial likelihood only if appropriate; benchmark against a duration-only/null model.
4. Independent temporal/episode-held-out forecasts and pre-frozen scoring (log loss or proper survival score); training-period covariates must be observable at forecast issue time and no future adoption share may leak into historical risk sets.
5. No claims about causal regime survival, human psychology or policy diffusion solely from association, and no E2/E3 research grade until the required authentic external evaluation is complete.

Keep R073 and PESEN v0.1 FROZEN; project order original R series first, then uniform classification, only then model fusion. **R172 is not yet accepted**, and no new human independent or historical external prediction has been claimed here.
