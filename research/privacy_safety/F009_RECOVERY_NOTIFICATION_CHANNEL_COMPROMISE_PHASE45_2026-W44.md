## Public primary standard
NIST SP 800-63B-4 account recovery and account notifications: https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-4/sp800-63b.html and https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-4/sp800-63b/events/ . Recovery triggers subscriber notification; CSP supports at least two notification addresses and sends to applicable addresses. A notice is a fraud-detection mechanism. Delivery does not itself authorize a high-impact restoration of erased data. NIST warns that email is unsuitable as an out-of-band authenticator, while email may be used for certain recovery codes and notifications under specified rules. The synthetic fresh-nonce gate and separate human review below are project candidate controls, not NIST mandatory specifications for erasure-ledger replay.

## Proposed controls and unresolved risks
Maintain separately versioned recovery authorization, deletion ledger, alias proof, credential revocation and notification transaction identity. Send notification across independently controlled routes where possible; require manual dispute path, bounded freshness and quarantine when evidence is missing or channels are compromised. Keep notification payloads minimal and exclude sensitive decision inputs. An attacker who controls both notification routes, the human reviewer or the original recovery authority is outside this finite model.
