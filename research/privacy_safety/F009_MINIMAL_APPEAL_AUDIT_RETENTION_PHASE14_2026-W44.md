## Primary sources and scope
NIST SP 800-63B-4 https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-4/sp800-63b/events/ requires account recovery notifications and user repudiation contact information. OWASP official logging guidance https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Logging_Cheat_Sheet.html advises avoiding directly logged tokens, passwords and sensitive personal data. UK ICO storage limitation guidance https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/data-protection-principles/a-guide-to-the-data-protection-principles/storage-limitation/ requires purpose-specific justified retention and review; it states pseudonymisation can remain personal data. ICO guidance is under review following UK law changes. These are primary standards/guidance, not a universal retention deadline or jurisdiction-specific legal opinion.

## Candidate control and risk
Store minimal event category, purpose, versioned status and bounded retention review date; keep actual evidence separately with access control and documented legal basis. A short opaque reference can still link to a person; never label it anonymous without proof. Retention expiry, withdrawal, dispute and verified legal hold require separate review paths. A deletion request should not silently disappear under an unsupported indefinite audit hold. An active appeal should not automatically justify retention; the toy gate requires an externally established verified basis. Review holds need expiration and human appeal, while real obligations differ by jurisdiction.
