## Candidate controls and unresolved risks
Maintain request identity, processing scope, backup inventory, time-limited review reason, access restriction and appeal pathway separately. Suspend high-impact automated decisions on disputed data. Do not assume a pending erasure request permits indiscriminate destruction of legally needed evidence. Do not automatically hold all data forever due to a restriction request.
