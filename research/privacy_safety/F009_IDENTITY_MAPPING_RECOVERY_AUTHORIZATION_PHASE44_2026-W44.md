## Threat and candidate controls
An apparently valid identity rotation map alone cannot restore deleted data. A valid erasure proof without independently authorized map reconstruction also cannot grant restore. Old backup identity and new account identity must be related with versioned evidence, separated authorization and revocation records, and a notification independent of the recovery channel. Evidence missing, invalid, or time-conflicted: quarantine and request human review. Restrict mapping storage by purpose, access, retention and deletion; avoid placing sensitive raw values in the audit ledger.
