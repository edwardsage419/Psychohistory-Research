## Direct publication sources identified and source-access gate

1. Michael Tomasello, Ann Cale Kruger and Hilary Horn Ratner (1993), [*Cultural learning*](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0140525X0003123X), *Behavioral and Brain Sciences* 16(3):495–552, Cambridge original article abstract/record and Duke publication page checked. Accounts for imitative, instructed and collaborative learning mechanisms and identifies high-fidelity cultural transmission as part of cumulative learning. Original author psychological measures and full text diagrams/claims are not yet reconstructed.
2. Claudio Tennie, Josep Call and Michael Tomasello (2009), [*Ratcheting up the ratchet: on the evolution of cumulative culture*](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19620111/), *Philosophical Transactions of the Royal Society B*, DOI to verify from journal record before freezing mathematical work. Original PubMed paper record and abstract checked; contrast between individual innovation and process-focused imitation/teaching. No source ape experimental trials examined.
3. Joseph Henrich (2004), [*Demography and cultural evolution: how adaptive cultural processes can produce maladaptive losses — the Tasmanian case*](https://doi.org/10.2307/4128416), *American Antiquity* 69(2):197–214. Original Cambridge journal abstract and print issue verified. Model hypothesizes how smaller pools of social learners may reduce the maintenance of difficult cultural skills, while acknowledging wider empirical interpretation. **The present audit makes no independent finding about actual Tasmanian skills, demographic causation or technology loss.**
4. Maxime Derex, Marie-Pauline Beugin, Bernard Godelle and Michel Raymond (2013), [*Experimental evidence for the influence of group size on cultural complexity*](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature12774), *Nature* 503:389–391. Original Nature publisher article, author list, 2013-11-13 publication date and original abstract directly checked. Reports a dual-task computer game involving human volunteers and the role of group size in cultural complexity. **Importantly the publisher page currently advertises three explicit downloadable "Source data" XLS files for Figures 1–3 and supplementary PDF materials.** None of these file bytes have yet been downloaded or checked. Their actual structure, participant/event granularity and out-of-sample feasibility remain unknown.
