## Frozen falsifiers and exact results

1. Exactly 48 CSV source records and 16 condition summaries, all reported attitudes in [-1,1].
2. All three mechanisms preserve initial attitude in 12 of 16 conditions with no autonomy or no initial mismatch.
3. DISSONANCE and RESPONSE_DEMAND give identical final reports in **16 of 16 conditions**, while assigned latent strain differs in **4 conditions**. Observable final reports alone cannot identify which constructed mechanism generated them.
4. In both counterattitudinal autonomous pairs, DISSONANCE's absolute attitude movement is **1** at justification 1 and **1/2** at justification 3. Exactly **2 strict low-justification-greater contrasts**.
5. In the same pairs, INCENTIVE movement is **1** at justification 1 and **3/2** at justification 3. Exactly **2 strict high-justification-greater contrasts**.
6. An independent auditor reenumerates all 48 rows and all 16 condition summaries and rejects two deliberate source alterations.

All six frozen conditions passed for the invented arithmetic. The matching reports between two mechanisms are guaranteed by their definitions. These constructed outcomes are mathematical counterexamples to identification from reported attitude alone, not empirical evidence of cognitive strain or demand effects.
