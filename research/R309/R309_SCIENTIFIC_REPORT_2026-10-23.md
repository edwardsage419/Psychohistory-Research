Five fictional agents share **21 exact integer (S,I,R) compartment counts** with total S+I+R=5. Nine frozen per-contact transmission/recovery parameter pairs p,q∈{0,1/2,1} create **189 source worlds**. In one simultaneous step, each of S originally susceptible receives independent p-probability contact exposure from each originally infected I; any success infects that susceptible. Each of the I **original** infectives recovers independently with probability q; new infections do not themselves infect or recover until the next step. This project formula gives per-susceptible attack probability a=1−(1−p)^I, X new infections∼Binomial(S,a), Y old recoveries∼Binomial(I,q), and next state (S−X,I+X−Y,R+Y).

All per-world feasible joint events x∈0..S and y∈0..I are recorded **including exact zero-probability boundary cases**, 1,134 complete source rows across 189 worlds, **not** 1,134 independent epidemics, infections or patients. Full exact rational probability per event is binomial(S,x;a) × binomial(I,y;q). Every world sums exactly to probability1 and conserves 5 persons. S never increases, R never decreases. Exact state expectation E[S']=S−Sa, E[I']=I+Sa−Iq and E[R']=R+Iq is independently recovered from full event row sums.

## Pre-frozen evidence and negative controls

For the artificial initial state (S,I,R)=(3,2,0) and p=q=1/2, the one-susceptible attack chance is **3/4** and conditional next expected counts are **(3/4, 13/4, 1)**. There remains a positive **1/256** artificial chance of *zero* new infections and *both* old infectives recovering, resulting in (3,0,2). This demonstrates how a one-step infection process can reach disease-free I=0 without infecting all susceptible agents **within this exact toy model**; it does not estimate a real extinction probability.

For p=q=1 at the same initial (3,2,0), every originally susceptible is infected and every old infective recovers, hence the deterministic next state is **(0,3,2)**—not the erroneous (0,0,5). The three *new* infectives have not recovered simultaneously. When I=0, the disease-free state is absorbing for all p,q. When p=0, no new infections occur; when q=0, original infectives cannot recover; with S=0,I=5,q=1 all recover and the process reaches (0,0,5). These negative and simultaneous-ordering controls are important when extrapolating to other mathematical forms: **no network topology, continuous-time hazards, serial time dynamics, duration, reproduction ratio R0 or multi-step prediction performance** has been demonstrated here.
