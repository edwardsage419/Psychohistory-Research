Each has three policies, OPEN, PRESSURE and AUDIT, giving **384 original artificial records**. OPEN chooses majority private signal; PRESSURE replaces public reports with the leader's position when pressure is active; AUDIT retains those reports but, when pressure and audit are both active, sets decision to an explicitly supplied perfect truth oracle. Truth is independently enumerated from signals and leadership, so this universe contains no realistic signal accuracy relationship.

## Exact prespecified falsifiers

1. **384** artificial source records and **128** condition summaries, all states binary.
2. Under pressure, **64/64** conditions display public unanimity, including **48** conditions with privately nonunanimous signals.
3. With pressure disabled, OPEN and PRESSURE agree on public reports and final decisions in **64/64** conditions.
4. With pressure enabled, the leader decision overrides the private majority in **32/64** conditions.
5. The perfect-information gate changes PRESSURE's choice in **16/32** pressure-plus-audit conditions. Overall artificial accuracy is OPEN **64/128**, PRESSURE **64/128**, AUDIT **80/128**.
6. Independent no-import auditor exactly reconstructs the complete CSV and JSON and rejects one deliberately changed CSV decision and one deliberately changed JSON accuracy cell.

All six conditions passed. The higher AUDIT accuracy is mechanically guaranteed by its perfect truth oracle, with no real-world intervention claim. Public unanimity conceals private disagreement in the constructed PRESSURE mapping by definition; this does not identify actual groupthink or show that any particular historical group exhibited it.
