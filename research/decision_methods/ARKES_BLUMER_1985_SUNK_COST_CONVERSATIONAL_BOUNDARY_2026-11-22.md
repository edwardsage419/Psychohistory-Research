## Distinct gap and dedup
Existing November 22 upfront-fee sequential sampling note is normative cost accounting. This note is descriptive: distinguishing a user's stated attachment to previous investment from genuine future-oriented preferences. Repository code searches for sunk cost, opportunity cost, Arkes Blumer and Thaler 1980 returned no hits; full semantic dedup remains unknown.
## Proposed minimal question and explanation
First provide a preliminary comparison. Only if historical expenditure is driving a sensitive recommendation, ask: “如果从今天重新选择，继续还要付出什么，停止能收回什么？过去投入是否改变了未来实际收益？”
Separate nonrecoverable outlays, refunds/resale/termination penalties, future incremental costs and benefits, user-confirmed commitments/identity/learning values, and unknowns. A past investment may causally change future skills or obligations; retain these effects when supported. Never label the user irrational solely for mentioning past investment.
## Competing explanations
Continuing may be rational due to acquired skills, termination penalties, improved information, endorsed promises, or changed values. Regret, loss framing and self-justification may generate similar language. Small and historical study contexts limit external validity.

8. Hard constraint prohibits continuation: feasibility first.
9. Earlier training increases future skill: retain evidence-supported causal effect.
10. Identical future consequences and preferences with different historical price: recommendation invariant.
