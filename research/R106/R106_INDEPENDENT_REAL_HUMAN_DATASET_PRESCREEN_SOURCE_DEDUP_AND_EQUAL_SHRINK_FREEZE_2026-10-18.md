Status: CANDIDATE_SELECTION_ONLY. No original human behavioral values downloaded or scored, no new E1/E2. PESEN v0.1 and R073 FROZEN.

## Why move to R106

Formal R105 2024 Montero-Porras defaults CPR experiment verified 412 human subjects, 103 fixed four-person groups, 10 rounds, 3708 scored next extraction shares, original MD5 and independent replay. Its frozen equal-weight shrinkage control defeated genuine original within-group peer-specific lag: MSE M2=0.04330681321947243 vs M3=0.04770729287359996, with 83/103 groups showing negative true-peer gain. This is an important outcome that limits the interpretation of R102's 8/8 genuine source sessions M3-vs-M1 gain because R102 lacked matched shrinkage M2. Neither source directly identifies causal social learning or R073 exact two-person opponent model. Source registry already flags R099 and R104 same original DOI, R095's Schniter/Shields 2020 Zenodo record 4321821, and R096's Montero-Porras 2022 Dryad record 10.5061/dryad.37pvmcvmk. These MUST NOT be counted as new independent cohorts if rediscovered under another title.

## Independent-source shortlisting 2026-10-18 (publisher metadata only)

Candidate A: **Drouvelis and Pearce 2025**, Zenodo [10.5281/zenodo.15462528](https://zenodo.org/records/15462528), published May 19 2025. Official dataset page lists `cleandata.dta` about 1.5 MB with public whole-source MD5 **`4cce04ea68b7a0492c242b206efebe53`**, and three Stata do-files with their own published MD5s. It is a replication of Proto, Rustichini and Sofianos (2022) `Intelligence and strategy choice in indefinitely repeated prisoner's dilemma games`. **Critical independent-cohort uncertainty:** its publisher description explicitly says 'You will also need to download the data files from the original study'. The deposited cleaned DTA may or may not include original investigators' subjects or a new human cohort; there is no basis yet to assign new actual humans or complete original session/opponent IDs. Freeze scientific admission rule: no R106 independent human E2 until source codebook and publication establish whether subjects were newly recruited and that its human cohort has no overlap with the original study. DTA source whole-MD5 can be verified without evaluating actions but is not a CSV and requires a safe DTA metadata implementation.

Protect against identical participants playing two sequential treatments or groups and source technical-session aliases.
3. Freeze exactly matched-shrinkage controls: M1 own lag; M2 fixed 0.5 own lag + 0.5 independent pooled contextual mean at t-1; M3 fixed 0.5 own lag + 0.5 actual previous opponent/peer lag; M4 fixed 0.5 own lag + 0.5 valid same-condition shuffled/donor source lag; all same heldout actors/rounds, no future test session leakage in pooled references.
4. Primary confirmatory quantity MSE(M2)−MSE(M3), with original whole session held out whenever source genuinely supports it. If only source fixed groups are exposed, call it LOOG and never LOSO. Replication claims must distinguish dyad from 3/5-peers and C/D Brier from contribution-fraction MSE.
5. Report all genuine independent source units and negative strata, plus agent feasibility and which behaviors were human or automatic, with source hash and exact double replay. No post-hoc model optimization, pseudoindependent p values or causal mechanisms.
