* Authors comment explicitly that `otherleft` is the other player's action in *current period t*, while `p2otherleft` is the other player's action at *previous period t−1*. These values require **independent original dyad reciprocity and timing checks** before predictive use, not unquestioned adoption as a prepared label. The authors use lagged opponent `p2otherleft` as a predictor at `practiceperiod>1`.
* The analytical match proxy `egen matchid=group(Session Group Period)` groups source session, pair/group and period. The scripts pair neighboring sorted records to obtain some opponent attributes. **This matching procedure does not on its own prove every actual row has exactly two mutual matched actors**; future source roster should check 2 persons in each exact source match key and unique person×event role.

They use `part==2` or `part==3` filters and `practiceperiod` first/later period boundaries.
