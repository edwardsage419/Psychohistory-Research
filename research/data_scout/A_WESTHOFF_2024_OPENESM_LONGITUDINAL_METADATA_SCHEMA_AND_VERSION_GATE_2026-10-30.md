## Scientific and access gate

Participant identifier: documented, stability across waves requires raw data validation. Time units: scheduled beep within day, raw timestamp; exact timezone, delays, missingness and duplicate participant-day-beep rows remain unverified. Behavior: self-reported item ratings, including positive_behavior_retention and negative_behavior_retention; not confirmed trial-by-trial objectively observed action or experimental devaluation. No independently randomized sessions are documented here. License: metadata claims CC BY 4.0 for harmonized dataset, subject to verification against exact Zenodo record. Accessibility: public metadata and public TSV listing; file download/parse NOT_RUN. Reproduction effort: moderate after obtaining bytes, require identity/time/missingness and as-of checks. Privacy: retain aggregate metadata, avoid redistributing identifiable timestamps or raw personal data until necessity and privacy review.
