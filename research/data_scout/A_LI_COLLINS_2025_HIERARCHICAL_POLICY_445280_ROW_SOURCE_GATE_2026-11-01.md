## Verified source and actual data structure
The peer-reviewed paper reports 1,026 human participants and over one million choices in hierarchical policy learning tasks. Actual full public data.csv was read through the GitHub repository connector as UTF-8 text and parsed in this run. Observed 445,280 trial rows, 14 columns, 1,012 distinct anonymous id values. Each observed id has exactly 440 rows. All id, block, trial keys are unique, and each id appears in only one experiment. Experiment 1 has 195,360 rows; Experiment 2 has 249,920 rows. Seven condition values occur: V1-V1, V1-V3, V3-V1, V3-V3, V1-V2, V2-V1, V2-V2. Blocks 1 through 12 and trial indices up to 60 observed. Actual fields: id, participant, experiment, condition, block, trial, s1, a1, r1, n_presses1, s2, a2, r2, n_presses2. Stage 1 and 2 action cells are empty on 20,581 rows each, consistent with README stating performance-dependent transitions in early blocks, although individual missingness causes remain untested. The action-list cells contain 816,745 and 818,106 semicolon-separated action tokens respectively, including potential invalid presses. These tokens are not independent participant decisions. No raw personal answers or identifiers were archived.

The 1,012 distinct CSV ids differ from the paper's 1,026 completed participants. Whether this is due to preprocessing or exclusion requires author methods and raw pickle comparison. Do not silently equate the two populations. No raw pickle was loaded, as it is large and untrusted pickle can execute code.

## R326 and R073 eligibility
High-value E2 candidate for longitudinal hierarchical policy acquisition and transfer, with participant identity, condition, block, trial, action and reward. Strong domain relevance, but frozen R326 is a three-group Bernoulli latent-regime toy with no direct mapping to the paper's two-stage state-action task. Any real-data fit or scoring requires a new frozen observation map, participant-level split and prespecified baseline. Shared V1-V1 conditions across experiments create possible overlap in task structure; use participant-disjoint and condition-aware evaluation. R073 eligibility HARD_NO_BY_TASK_TYPE because these are sequential individual hierarchical-policy decisions rather than continuous 100-round human dyadic prisoner's dilemma.
