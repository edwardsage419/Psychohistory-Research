## Actually verified on publisher page
Publisher's Methods section describes US data from 1940 to 2016 for the three empirical predictions. Inputs include wage variance 1980–2016, Census income share tables 1967–2020, law graduate income data, and political stress proxy measures. These source series have distinct definitions, dates, units and measurement provenance. They cannot be assumed to be a homogeneous annual event-level panel.

The publisher's Supporting Information list contains **eight TIF figure files S1–S8 and one XLSX S1 Table** (DOI suffix .s009). The XLSX is described as regression coefficients for PSI and demonstrations with t statistics, not a full original annual observation table. The eight images are figure-level materials. We inspected the article HTML and supporting-file descriptions; direct XLSX byte retrieval was unsuccessful, so sheet names, exact cell values, underlying observation rows and license for third-party source datasets were not checked.

## Data suitability and blockers
Candidate level: published historical macro evidence, potentially E3 after original series reconstruction, unit harmonization and frozen out-of-sample evaluation. The published figures and coefficient table alone do not provide verified year-by-year individual MMP, EMP, SFD, political unrest outcomes and independent training/test periods. The study is a competing empirical critique; its outcome definitions cannot be silently mapped to R157's artificial unit-scale lag components.
