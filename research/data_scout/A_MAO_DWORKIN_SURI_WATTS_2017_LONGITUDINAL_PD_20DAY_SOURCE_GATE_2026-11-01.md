## Experimental design verified from article
Initial recruitment 113 participants, 94 met completion criterion. Twenty weekdays, August 4 to August 31, 2015. Two daily sessions with stable membership and repeated anonymous random pairing. Each session had 20 supergames, each exactly 10 consecutive rounds, with a new random partner for the next supergame. Participants completing the study played on average about 375 supergames, 3,720 individual decisions each. Article reports 374,251 individual decisions collectively. Round-level C/D choices and opponent feedback exist in the study design, with day/session/supergame/round hierarchy. Payoffs T=7,R=5,P=3,S=1. Source code has NUMROUNDS=10 and NUMGAMES=20. Article says data are available on OSF; no confirmed downloadable file names, checksums, per-file license, anonymized participant ID schema, duplicate count, or actual row count from source bytes.

## R073 frozen compatibility
R073 requires 100 consecutive rounds in a single two-player treatment with fixed t=3..100 features, LOSO across independent sessions, and exactly the frozen M0..M6 scoring and alpha=20 smoothing. This study's interaction episodes are ten rounds each, followed by random rematching. Its thousands of participant decisions cannot be concatenated across games into a single 100-round fixed-partner episode. R073 strict direct-transfer qualification: HARD_NO_BY_EPISODE_LENGTH_AND_REMATCHING.
