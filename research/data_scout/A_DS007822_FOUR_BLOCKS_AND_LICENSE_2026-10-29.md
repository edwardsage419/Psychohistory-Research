# A source audit: ds007822 temporal structure and upstream license
Date: 2026-10-29 UTC. Target: (A,ds007822 v1.0.0,40 trial time structure and reuse rights).

The original peer-reviewed English data article by Kim, Jun and Nam (Data in Brief 68, 113064, 2026-07-10; https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13427532/) explicitly describes 11 triads, 33 people, 40 trials per person, and a task organized into four repeated blocks of ten trials. Each trial includes instruction, 4-second decision, 2-second feedback and rest. The 40 decisions are repeated within participant and triad, and four blocks are part of one session design; they must not be counted as 40 independent experiments. The article specifies 40x3 behavioral choice matrices with 1=cooperate, 2=defect, and 300 Hz EEG. The public dataset DOI is https://doi.org/10.18112/openneuro.ds007822.v1.0.0. The paper also lists upstream Figshare DOI https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.32197038.
