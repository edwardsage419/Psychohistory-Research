## Verified public source facts

Original paper Methods: 4 x 4 periodic lattice, 16 humans per session, 50 rounds per session, 9 sessions, 144 participants. Five sessions with payoff information (80 people), four sessions without payoff information (64 people). Each player chooses one action each round and interacts with four fixed lattice neighbors. Round unit is a simultaneous human action round, not a dyadic supergame; the design includes treatment and session boundaries. Expected participant-round opportunities from design = 144 x 50 = 7200, NOT verified actual file rows. Original Dryad page lists DataSet.csv (215.22 KB) and README_for_DataSet.txt (1.41 KB), publication July 27 2020.
