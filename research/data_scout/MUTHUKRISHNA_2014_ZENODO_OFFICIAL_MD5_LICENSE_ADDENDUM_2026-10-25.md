## Eligibility and measurement boundaries

Experiment 1 studies image-editing skill; experiment 2 studies knot-tying skill. These are real human cultural learning outcomes, with potential group/chain dependence and generation as the time unit. The existing data-scout audit previously described 100 observations per experiment and ten chain IDs in experiment 2. This round did not independently recount rows or verify identities, and therefore does not promote those prior counts to fresh byte-level evidence.

The dataset does not provide R073's bilateral repeated prisoner's dilemma actions and cannot be scored with R073's frozen rules. It also does not directly measure R164's binary content salience multiplier, so it is not eligible for direct R164 model reproduction without a prospective measurement mapping and a separate holdout protocol.
