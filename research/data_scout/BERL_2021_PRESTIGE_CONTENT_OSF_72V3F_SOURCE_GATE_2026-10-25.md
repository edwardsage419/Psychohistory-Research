## Verified metadata and unit
The paper reports 163 real participants, 72 UK and 91 US. Two artificial narrative stories (Muki 265 propositions and Taka & Toro 273 propositions), with high/low prestige regional accent cue conditions and proposition-level recall as a binary outcome. Reported total presented propositions: 87,421; recalled: 12,505. Article statistical model uses participant and proposition random effects. These are repeated within-participant proposition outcomes; 87,421 rows, if recovered, would not represent 87,421 independent people. Published text reports a 33-recording subset re-coded for intercoder agreement, Cohen's kappa 0.737. The article is CC BY 4.0; separate OSF data-file reuse terms remain unverified.
