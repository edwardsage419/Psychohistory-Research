## New provenance decision
The Mendeley CC BY-NC 3.0 license applies to the Centipede record. It cannot be transferred to the Leicester Figshare Prisoner's Dilemma package, which is explicitly All Rights Reserved. The Mendeley exit-point observations cannot be substituted for Colman 300-round dyadic C/D sequences or the original R073 Montero 2022 source. Two DOI identifiers, two game definitions, two repositories, and two license regimes must remain separate. This prevents an apparent free-license workaround from silently changing the study.
