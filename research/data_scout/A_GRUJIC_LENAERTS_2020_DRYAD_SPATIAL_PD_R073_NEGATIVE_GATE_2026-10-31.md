The paper's methods explicitly describe nine separate experimental sessions, each with 16 human participants on a 4 by 4 periodic lattice, for 144 people total. Every session lasted 50 rounds. Five sessions and 80 participants had neighbors' payoff information (TWI); four sessions and 64 participants did not (TWO). Each person chose one action per round, simultaneously interacting with four fixed spatial neighbors. Each round is the time unit. The same participant's action affects four dyadic games, so those four interactions must not be counted as independent human action choices. The 50-round session, not the individual lattice edge, is the natural independent experimental cluster; participant-level longitudinal identity and lattice position must be reconstructed from original data documentation before scoring.
