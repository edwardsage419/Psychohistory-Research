## Prior evidence already on main
R093 Phase A/B/C independently froze data source and behavior evaluation before scoring, checked the exact 612,196-byte public CSV, MD5 and SHA256 4eb3380a5eacdef5466709b343af391b27b48a35b5b82f355a4d85d5172644bc. It recorded 21,195 raw CSV rows, including 83 rows without experiment identity; 1,508 participants in six experimental treatments, each with 14 rounds, and 21,112 identified person-round rows. R093 reported 16,365 eligible adjacent-round outcomes across 1,371 participants and 137 participants with no eligible pair. Its archived result compared participant-held-out M0/M1/M2 with mean squared losses 0.106708344 / 0.073090131 / 0.063657960. These numbers are EXISTING E2 evidence, not independently recomputed this run.
R093 notes the original study's automatic replacement for missed decisions, unobserved at individual choice level. Participant presence alone cannot establish that each recorded action was manually chosen. Six common-environment treatments are the main independent experimental units. Its M0 uses other participants' SAME-round contributions, so it is not a real-time whole-group prospective forecast. The original R093 scope is CLOSED_BOUNDED, not a new external independent experiment for R073.
