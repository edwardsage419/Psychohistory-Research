* Bandura (2000) *Exercise of Human Agency Through Collective Efficacy*, DOI https://doi.org/10.1111/1467-8721.00064: perceived **shared collective capability beliefs**.
* Sampson, Raudenbush & Earls (1997), *Neighborhoods and Violent Crime: A Multilevel Study of Collective Efficacy*, *Science* 277:918–924, DOI https://doi.org/10.1126/science.277.5328.918: 8782 Chicago resident survey respondents in343 neighborhoods; social cohesion and willingness to intervene; **adjusted association**, not a newly reproduced causal effect here.
* Goddard, Hoy & Woolfolk Hoy (2000), *Collective Teacher Efficacy: Its Meaning, Measure, and Impact on Student Achievement*, DOI https://doi.org/10.3102/00028312037002479: original teacher efficacy measure and school outcome associations, neither reanalyzed nor replicated here.
