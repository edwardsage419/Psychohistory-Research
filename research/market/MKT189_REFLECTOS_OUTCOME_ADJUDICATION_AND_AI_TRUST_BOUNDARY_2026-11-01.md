Inputs described by vendor: decision text/context/rationale, confidence, projected outcomes, outcome checkpoints, risk, workspace membership, optional calendar events, optional AI interactions. State: decision record, due/review status, confidence and outcome. Feedback: 30/90/180-day checkpoint review and calibration claims; algorithm and missingness assumptions undisclosed. Goal: improve decision judgement; no demonstrated external causal benefit from inspected sources. Explainability: UI shows calibration and risk claims; exact computation undisclosed.

Official homepage FAQ says decision content is inaccessible even to Reflect OS. Security page says encrypted at rest, decrypted within server-side Edge Functions for AI/PDF, with plaintext never leaving the trusted environment. Privacy policy Sections 2.2 and 5 explicitly say relevant decision text and context are sent to Anthropic API for inference when AI is enabled; Section 4 lists Anthropic as US subprocessor. This is a material trust-boundary ambiguity requiring a clarified data-flow diagram and definitions of trusted environment and accessible. It is not evidence of a breach or misuse. API inference and model training are different operations. Vendor says no AI training and AI features can be disabled; neither was independently tested. Privacy policy: 30-day post-closure decision deletion, 30-day backup overwrite, 12-month audit log, 30-day AI log before anonymisation. Access controls and exact retention enforcement not tested.
