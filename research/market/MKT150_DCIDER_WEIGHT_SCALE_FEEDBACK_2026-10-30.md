## Researcher-created falsifiable toy
Script research/market/mkt150_dcider_weight_scale_counterfactual_20261030.js, pure JS with 27 V8 checks PASS. Two fictional alternatives A=(9,4) and B=(6,8) on two criteria. The researcher's weighted sum with importance 0.6/0.4 yields A=7, B=6.8. With 0.5/0.5 it yields A=6.5, B=7. Exact switch at weight of criterion one 4/7. Holding 0.6/0.4 but doubling only criterion-two raw scores reverses ranking: A=8.6, B=10. This is a scale sensitivity counterexample for an assumed weighted-sum formula; it is NOT the actual Dcider algorithm or proof of product failure.
Feedback identifiability: two fictional worlds have the same recorded choice A and observed success 1; unchosen B success is 0 in one and 1 in the other. The causal advantage of choosing A is 1 or 0. Logging only chosen outcomes cannot distinguish the worlds. A user-visible learning loop requires separately identifying counterfactual assumptions and selection effects.

## Candidate method and limitations
Preserve value version, raw criterion units, normalization rule, alternative set, predecision weights, decision time, feedback collection method, missingness and postdecision edits. Treat choice-score sensitivity as a range rather than a universal life utility. Record user autonomy and override; separate observed outcome, subjective satisfaction and unobserved counterfactual.
