## English original studies

McGuire (1961), *Resistance to Persuasion Conferred by Active and Passive Prior Refutation of the Same and Alternative Counterarguments*, *Journal of Abnormal and Social Psychology* 63:326–332, DOI 10.1037/h0048344. McGuire and Papageorgis (1961), *The Relative Efficacy of Various Types of Prior Belief-Defense in Producing Immunity Against Persuasion*, *Journal of Abnormal and Social Psychology* 62:327–337, DOI 10.1037/h0042026. Papageorgis and McGuire (1961), *The Generality of Immunity to Persuasion Produced by Pre-exposure to Weakened Counterarguments*, *Journal of Abnormal and Social Psychology* 62:475–481, DOI 10.1037/h0048430. The studies distinguish prior counterargument/refutation conditions, attack similarity and subsequent belief resistance. They are human experimental sources, but no participant-level source data has been recovered for project E2.
