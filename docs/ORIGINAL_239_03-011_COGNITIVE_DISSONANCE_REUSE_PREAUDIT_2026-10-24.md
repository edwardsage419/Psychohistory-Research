Festinger and Carlsmith (1959), *Cognitive Consequences of Forced Compliance*, *Journal of Abnormal and Social Psychology* 58:203–210, DOI 10.1037/h0041593, original full text hosted by MIT https://web.mit.edu/curhan/www/docs/Articles/15341_Readings/Motivation/required_reading/3Festinger_Carlsmith_1959_Cognitive_consequences_of_forced_compliance.pdf . ## Existing R scope inspected

R291 01-013 Theory of Reasoned Action: synthetic attitudes, norms, intention and behavior conditional mediation. No induced compliance dissonance or post behavior attitude revision.

R292 01-014 Theory of Planned Behavior: synthetic perceived and actual control, feasibility and behavior. No inconsistency pressure or dissonance reduction mechanism.

R323 01-015 Value Belief Norm Theory: artificial latent norm mediation and observational nonidentification. No cognitive inconsistency or counterattitudinal action manipulation.

R221 09-012 Causal Mediation: six artificial structural models with observational mediation aliases. Useful for the warning that behavioral or mediator associations do not identify psychological mechanisms; not a direct Festinger model.

R232 10-006 SEM: artificial measurement and latent confounding counterexamples. Again a methodological analogy, no strict reuse of dissonance dynamics.

## Candidate falsifiable next gate

Before creating R334 artificial data, inspect early ZIPs and adjacent R series for a specific counterattitudinal action, external justification, and subsequent attitude revision mapping. If none strictly reproduces the required finite mechanism, freeze a small finite, falsifiable protocol. A possible constructed design enumerates initial attitude, imposed action, and external justification, with dissonance pressure changing the magnitude of an invented attitude update. A separate direct incentive or demand effect comparator should be able to produce matching final attitudes in some worlds. Exact alternatives and counts must be fixed before computing outcomes. Such a study would establish only mathematical E1; neither the 1959 experiment nor the whole theory would be replicated.
