## Primary English sources

Petty, R. E. and Cacioppo, J. T. (1986), *The Elaboration Likelihood Model of Persuasion*, *Advances in Experimental Social Psychology* 19:123–205, DOI 10.1016/S0065-2601(08)60214-2. Original author PDF https://richardepetty.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/1986-advances-pettycacioppo.pdf . Petty, Cacioppo and Schumann (1983), *Central and Peripheral Routes to Advertising Effectiveness: The Moderating Role of Involvement*, *Journal of Consumer Research* 10(2):135–146, DOI 10.1086/208954. Their human experiment crossed involvement, argument strength and endorser cues; argument quality was more influential at high involvement, endorser cues more influential at low involvement. Petty, Cacioppo and Goldman (1981), *Personal Involvement as a Determinant of Argument-Based Persuasion*, *Journal of Personality and Social Psychology* 41(5):847–855, DOI 10.1037/0022-3514.41.5.847. These original studies do not provide participant-level data in this repository.

The 1986 account concerns a continuum of elaboration and multiple roles for variables. A binary central/peripheral switch is an illustrative restricted model and cannot stand for the entire ELM or prove that low involvement always suppresses argument effects.
