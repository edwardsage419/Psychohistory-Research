## Frozen ORIGINAL TO THIS PROJECT finite SES model (not authors' equations)
Two synthetic resource users SHARE an integer stock S ∈ {0,1,...,8}, physical carrying cap8, starting stock S0=6. The two users are assumed perfectly compliant with their announced quota and divide each year's realized gross harvest equally; NO individual incentive equilibrium or measured enforcement is claimed. This is a closed synthetic resource accounting mechanism. Horizon T=8. A zero stock is absorbing ecological-collapse state: no harvest or natural regrowth can exit 0. Resource system RS=stock/environment; units RU=integer extracted units; users U=two symmetric users; GS=harvest rule and its annual infrastructure operation; feedback is rule conditioned on prior stock and changes in physical resource. Governance provider is represented ONLY by an exogenous independent infrastructure-failure indicator; providers' strategic choice excluded.

Three policies and frozen intended gross planned harvest per time step given the PRE-PERIOD stock S:
- OPEN: min(S,2) units (two users each normally request 1).
- QUOTA: min(S,1) units regardless S≥1.
- ADAPT: planned 0 if S≤2, 1 if 3≤S≤6, 2 if 7≤S≤8. This *feedback policy* changes requests in response to current observed stock; monitoring stock is assumed perfect.
Annual infrastructure breakdown F_t is independent Bernoulli(f) with f∈{0,1/4}. If F_t=1 AND policy≠OPEN, intended harvest is REPLACED by OPEN for that year, with fee still charged. For OPEN failure is behaviorally irrelevant. Annual extreme climate shock D_t∈{0,2} independent Bernoulli(q) with q∈{0,1/4,1/2}. F_t and D_t independent across periods and from each other. Base extraction is min(S,planned); shock follows extraction. Set B=max(0,S−H−D); regrowth G(B)=1 if 1≤B≤6 else 0. Next stock S'=min(8,B+G(B)); special S=0 absorbing. **No other stochasticity, no ocean migration, no fish age, and no endogenous best response to incentives.**
Annual governance operation/maintenance expense for all eight years charged regardless infrastructure failure: OPEN=0; QUOTA=1/8; ADAPT=1/4. Cumulative harvest is sum H, social net=E[sum H]−T*cost, no time discount, no intrinsic ecosystem utility; survival criterion stock S8>0. Stock and fees in stylized arbitrary units; net is not true societal welfare because ecosystem services ignored. Do not assert ecological survival implies net-profit maximal, or vice versa.
