## Candidate independently published original source

Poncela-Casasnovas et al., *Humans display a reduced set of consistent behavioral phenotypes in dyadic games*, Science Advances 2 (2016), DOI **10.1126/sciadv.1600451**, [Zenodo original record 1127154](https://zenodo.org/records/1127154), record DOI **10.5281/zenodo.1127154**. The publication's source summary states **45 genuine separate experiments/replicas** over two days, each with a different set of human participants, usually 10 to 25 people and 13 to 18 rounds. Each individual plays dyadic games with varying payoff parameter S/T, original action C/D and original opponent ID and opponent action per round. These are source-description claims until independently verified from actual original row identities. The source authorship team is different from R102's main team but includes **Ángel Sánchez in both R102 and R103**; research samples/collectors and experiment rules differ. This is independent *experiment/data source* evidence, not fully disjoint co-authorship.

The first-party publicly named source is **`drbrain_decisions.csv`** with official publisher MD5 **`712e3a4ae905af3e004b4a31a38b250d`**, approximately 319.5 KB; paired anonymized user/codebook metadata `drbrain_users.csv` published MD5 `4f9862f29596deea80a20a5f35fdd8eb` exists, but **not authorized for download in Phase A**.
