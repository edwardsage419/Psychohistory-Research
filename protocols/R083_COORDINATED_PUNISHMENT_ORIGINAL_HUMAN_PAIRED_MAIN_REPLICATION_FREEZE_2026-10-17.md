## Frozen primary within-person estimand and competing accounts

Define `A=conditional_other_nopun` (same participant says punish when third-party does not punish) and `B=conditional_other_pun` (same participant says punish when third-party punishes). All comparisons condition on original experiment's own actor eligible `punisher==1`. After above gates, for each original `rerun` cohort separately count exact paired cells:
 `n00` (A0,B0), `n01` (A0,B1), `n10` (A1,B0), `n11` (A1,B1).
Compute n, `pA=(n10+n11)/n`, `pB=(n01+n11)/n`, `paired_mean_diff=pB-pA=(n01-n10)/n` with absolute formula cross-check. Exact two-sided conditional McNemar sign-test p, for discordants `D=n01+n10>0`: `p= min(1,2*sum(comb(D,j) for j<=min(n01,n10))/2**D)`; when D=0 define p=1, and report exact discordants. This is paired **elicited conditional strategy behavior**; `B-A` does not estimate an exogenous sanction *institution* policy treatment effect.

Compare `M0`: the rival proposition "participant's elicited sanctioning decision is independent of what they are told others will do", implies zero mean B-A; `M1`: conditional coordinated sanctioning preference implies positive mean B-A; `M2`: anti-coordination preference implies negative mean B-A. These are formal testable sign-level mechanisms, not fitted parameters or machine learning losses. Report all cells, both positive and negative individual switchers, even if M1 wins in one cohort and loses in the other. Pre-declared directional transfer gate: **both original independent cohorts have n>0 and paired_mean_diff>0**; else scientific transfer NOT SUPPORTED. Inference restricted to those samples and strategy questions. Actual author cohort independence should be separately checked in author documentation, and separate `rerun` code alone does not prove independent participants.

## Mandatory subgroup and negative-case checks

Stratification is **descriptive only**, not necessarily randomized; if the source has no individuals in a stratum, publish n=0 and metrics as null. Report two-cohort agreement/reversal of direction; do not pool the two before primary results.
