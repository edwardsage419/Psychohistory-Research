## Fixed finite world and exogenous source
Hidden state H is fixed in {0,1} over the two-decision horizon. Initial belief b=P(H=1) is one of 0/8,1/8,...,8/8. An agent may GUESS_0 or GUESS_1, ending immediately with reward 1 if correct and 0 otherwise, or SENSE once, paying exactly 1/8, observing Y in {0,1} with P(Y=H)=q and then making a terminal guess. Two observation-accuracy worlds q in {1/2,3/4}. All 9 beliefs x 2 accuracies x 2 hidden states x 2 observation labels form exactly 72 original artificial source rows, with exact joint mass P(H,Y|b,q). Zero-mass rows retained as labelled counterfactual source states. No human sample.

## Prespecified calculations and falsifiers
For each (b,q), compute the immediate terminal value M=max(b,1-b). Compute Bayesian posterior P(H=1|Y=y) using the known q and b, and expected sense value S=-1/8+sum_y P(Y=y) max(P(H=0,Y=y)/P(Y=y),P(H=1,Y=y)/P(Y=y)). A zero-probability observation has posterior NULL and contributes zero. Optimal value V=max(M,S). Tie-break GUESS_0 before GUESS_1 before SENSE, with SENSE selected only if strictly better than M.
