## Frozen mathematical model
One risk-neutral principal and one risk-averse agent with `U(w,a)=sqrt(w)-c_a`, nonnegative wages and utility units. Unobserved binary effort a∈{L,H}, c_L=0, c_H=1/4, external reservation utility `u0=1/2`. Public production output Y ∈{success,failure}: P(Y=success|H)=3/4, P(success|L)=1/4. Principal observed output pays nonnegative wage w_y, high-effort payoff revenue 2 if success, 0 otherwise. **Weak incentive compatibility** IC_H: E_H[sqrt(w)]−1/4≥E_L[sqrt(w)], **participation** IR_H:E_H[sqrt(w)]−1/4≥1/2. When indifference under weak IC assume chosen H, else high effort does not follow; explicitly mark this critical tie-break. Let `v_i=sqrt(w_i)≥0`; fixed-H cost is exact convex quadratic `C_H=min Σ pH_i v_i²` under linear IR and IC; reject floating-point/boolean, calculate Fraction rational. When H observable, only IR matters: optimal full insurance wage `(u0+c_H)^2=9/16`, resource cost 9/16.
Base two outcome high-action contract analytic exact solve; expected optimal wage and principal profit to be determined by program without freezing post-hoc answers. Low effort option optimal risk sharing constant `v_i=u0`, cost1/4, expected revenue1/2, profit1/4 (assuming agent L chosen; no IC_H).
 
## Additional signals fixed before scoring
1. **Redundant binary monitor** Z∈{good,bad} conditional on effort AND output is fair coin; P_H on (success,good),(success,bad),(failure,good),(failure,bad)=(3/8,3/8,1/8,1/8), P_L=(1/8,1/8,3/8,3/8). Verify P_H(Y),P_L(Y) as base. Signal adds no information on action conditional on output: likelihood ratio pH/pL constant within each Y. Prove optimal wage costs identical to base by Jensen's inequality and direct feasible clone contract. Verify no strict decrease, without relying on an insufficient best-effort numeric local solve.
2. **Informative binary monitor**, joint high distribution `(1/2,1/4,1/16,3/16)`; low `(1/16,3/16,1/4,1/2)` in same four-cell order. Verify output marginals unchanged (H success3/4, L success1/4) and distinguish conditional likelihood ratios for good vs bad within Y; solve constrained convex quadratic for best high effort. Strictly compare reduced optimal high-effort expected wage against base using **exact Fraction**, and issue primal feasibility and KKT-based globally optimal dual certificate.
3. **Monitoring fee comparison** fixed F∈{0,1/64,1/32,1/16,1/8,1/4}; principal's net H profit `3/2-C_H-F` vs unaided H profit `3/2-C_H(base)` and unaided L profit1/4. Fee paid to independent monitor, not wage, and does not change agent IR/IC. Freeze interpretation of strict and equal ties and explicit threshold F*=Cbase−Cinformative.
4. **Cost-of-effort sensitivity** c_H∈{0,1/8,1/4,3/8}, reservation u0=1/2 and same output probabilities, with base 2-state model and informative 4-state model, all solve rationally. At c_H=0, IC weak and full insurance can tie; do not generalize value of informative signals always strictly positive. Track first-best vs second-best gap and whether stronger incentives demand greater variance.
5. **Output-informativeness negative control** pH=pL (both 1/2,1/2), c_H=1/4: implementing H under hidden action is infeasible as `E_H v−E_L v=0 <1/4`. Return explicit infeasible, do not conflate infeasibility with numerical optimizer error.
