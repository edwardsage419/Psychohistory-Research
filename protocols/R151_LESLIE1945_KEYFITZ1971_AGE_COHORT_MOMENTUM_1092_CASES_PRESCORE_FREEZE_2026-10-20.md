## Frozen base 3-class female-equivalent normalized synthetic population
State at integer t: x_t=(J_t,A_t,O_t) nonnegative rational juvenile, reproductive adult and older cohort numbers, with one discrete time step per age class, TERMINAL older class departs at next step. No sexes, no births from juveniles, no open-ended longevity, no deaths within a step other than non-survivors, no density-dependence, no random life histories. These are numerical cohort-equivalent units, not empirically measured individuals.
Baseline Leslie-like age projection L:
J_{t+1}= 1*A_t + (1/2)*O_t;
A_{t+1}= (3/4)*J_t;
O_{t+1}= (2/3)*A_t.
All expected counts are Fraction with no rounding. Adults/older produce expected births during step; newborns enter J at next step. Juvenile survivors advance to A, adult survivors advance to O, old have no next class. Per-step accounting: births B=A+O/2, deaths D=J/4+A/3+O, net migrants M=0. Exact N(t+1)=N(t)+B-D+M. This is an artificial REPLACEMENT-EIGENVALUE (=1) Leslie matrix; it is not a TFR=2.1 statement, and no actual country fertility is represented. Stable stationary composition normalized w=(4/9,1/3,2/9); left reproductive-value vector v=(1,4/3,1/2), v·w=1; for baseline L v·x_t conserved. Characteristic polynomial (λ−1)(λ+1/2)^2; deterministic non-Perron modes decay (possibly t*(-1/2)^t), so N(t) tends to C=v·x0. Explicit exact finite-horizon closed form:
N_t=C + (a+b*t)*(-1/2)^t,
a=N0−C, b=3*C−N0−2*N1.
