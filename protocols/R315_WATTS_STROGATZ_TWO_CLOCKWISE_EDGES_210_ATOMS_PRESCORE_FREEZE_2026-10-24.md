## Input and randomized event order frozen before outcomes

1. Ring lattice nodes {0,...,N−1}, even N in {8,10}; undirected edges {i,i+1 mod N} and {i,i+2 mod N}, unordered and unique, giving mean degree k=4 and E=2N.
2. For each N independently, beta=p∈{0,1/2,1}. Exactly the first TWO clockwise nearest neighbor **distinct source edges** are processed in order (active,old_target)=(0,1), then (1,2), respecting the original paper's clockwise nearest-neighbor sweep. Stop after second event. Do not process (0,2) second because that belongs to the next lap.
3. At each edge with probability 1−p, keep the existing edge. With probability p choose a NEW target uniformly from vertices unequal to active and currently **not adjacent** to active before removing the old source edge; this forbids duplicate connections, self-loops and reselecting the old endpoint in the rewire branch. Remove old edge, add new one. There are positive candidates in each enumerated branch. These rules define the exact finite implementation; alternative libraries may differ in handling the original endpoint, and may not be treated as equivalent.
4. Enumerate every retain and allowable rewire target, including zero-probability alternatives, in deterministic increasing target order. Stage-one branches are 4 for N=8 and 6 for N=10. Stage-two conditional branches vary after prior edge removal: initial keep -> 4 or 6 branches; each initial rewire -> 5 or 7 branches. Number of potential source atoms per beta: N=8, 4+4+3×5=23; N=10, 6+6+5×7=47. Across three p values: **210 source event atoms**, and **180 complete two-event paths** (N8 19 per beta; N10 41 per beta).
5. Stage-one and stage-two weights must sum to exactly 1 for each context (including zero probabilities); the complete 2-event path masses must sum to exactly 1 per (N,p). All weights rational. Graph states remain simple with N fixed and E fixed. No edge disappearance, loop, or duplicate. Track each path's final edge set, exact probability, C (mean local triangle closure, zero for degree <2), connected component count, and L (mean shortest undirected graph distance over all unordered vertex pairs if connected, otherwise null). Do not silently drop disconnected pairs.
6. For p=0 every non-retain path probability=0, and the ring lattice remains unchanged. For p=1 all retain-containing paths have zero probability, all permissible rewiring choices conditional on the current graph are equally likely.
7. Pre-specified sentinels: both baselines connected with C>0; for p=0 the entire probability mass remains on the original graph. A rewire changes the degree of the old target and new target by −1 and +1, while keeping active degree unchanged. One rewire can increase or decrease the shortest path or clustering; do not claim both global metrics monotone in every individual event.
