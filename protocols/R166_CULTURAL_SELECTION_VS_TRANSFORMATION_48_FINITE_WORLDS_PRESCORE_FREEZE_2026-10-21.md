## 48 frozen fully finite Markov worlds
Artificial cultural tokens A=1 and B=0. Fixed N∈{4,8}; start A count for N4 k∈{0,1,2,4}, and for N8 k∈{0,2,4,8}. Exactly 3 regimes in each:
* neutral: selection weights wA:wB = 1:1; transformation (a,b)=(0,0).
* favors A: selection 2:1; transformation (a,b)=(1/3,0).
* favors B: selection 1:2; transformation (a,b)=(0,1/3).
Two distinctly defined mechanisms for each regime:
(SEL) select tokens from previous-generation model carriers with weights wA and wB. Under p=k/N, each new A indicator is Bernoulli(q_sel), \(q_{sel}=w_Ap/(w_Ap+w_B(1-p))\).
(TRF) copy a demonstrator uniformly, then change copied B to A with chance a, or copied A to B with chance b. \(q_{trf}=p(1-b)+(1-p)a\).
Each next generation N indicators are conditionally independent and K(t+1) is Binomial(N,q). Exactly **2 sizes ×4 starting counts ×3 regimes ×2 mechanisms =48** scenarios, each with exact entire distribution K_t=0..N for t=0..4 inclusive, plus count means. There are exactly **12 full transition matrices** saved (2 sizes×3 regimes×2 mechanisms). All frequencies and probabilities stored as Fraction strings.

## Two independent exact computational oracles
(1) Closed Fraction q formulas + integer binomial coefficients and row-vector matrix evolution.
(2) Independently enumerate each child's binary copying model and (for TRF) conditional transformation branches analytically from four joint copy-change outcomes, then enumerate the full 2^N next-generation binary outcome strings and propagate separately without method(1)'s probability or kernel. The independent child q for SEL is the weighted probability of copying an A carrier; for TRF use \(p(1-b)+(1-p)a\) evaluated by branch counts. This independent method is an *arithmetic oracle*, not a second empirical data source. All 48×5 distributions must exactly agree.

## Prespecified identification and negative controls
1. For every N at starting p=1/2, selection with weights 2:1 and directed B-to-A transformation at a=1/3 both give q=2/3 and **identical one-generation full binomial count distributions**. In the B direction, selection 1:2 versus A-to-B rate b=1/3 both give q=1/3. Thus a single count transition from the exact symmetric start cannot identify which mechanism operated.
2. When p=1/4, selection 2:1 gives q=2/5, while A-directed transformation a=1/3 gives q=1/2. The two mechanisms disagree at another experimental starting state, and their one-step kernels differ.
3. At p=0 in the A-favoring regime, selection cannot create missing A (q=0) but transformation produces q=1/3. At p=1, B-favoring selection cannot create B but transformation produces q=2/3.
4. With neutral weights and zero transformation, SEL and TRF **must coincide with neutral binomial cultural drift**, matching R165 μ=0 exact transition kernels for N4 and N8. This is a cross-stage reference, and should be explicitly checked by tests when running in the repository.
5. For N4 initially k=2 in A-favoring regime, both mechanisms have identical time-1 count distributions but **generally diverge in time-2 full distributions** as the nonlinear selection q_sel differs from affine q_trf at k≠N/2. Record exact first differing entry and TV distance, with `TV=1/2 Σ_j|P_sel(K2=j)−P_trf(K2=j)|`. Do not prespecify its sign or numerical value; report it as an execution result.
6. No-innovation selection always has 0 and N absorbing. Directed transformation with one-way mutation has the favored endpoint absorbing but not the opposing endpoint, so it has different eventual absorption properties. Do not assert observed groups actually undergo directional innovation.
7. Neutral SEL and TRF preserve E[K_t/N]=k0/N; selected or transformed nonneutral processes may change it. The label 'favors' only denotes assumed weights/transition rates, **not measured content quality, real fitness, preference or knowledge**.
8. More than one parameterization can fit the first transition at p=1/2. Success requires observing other starting frequencies, interventions or direct micro-observations. The original 2021 model used real-valued traits and stochastic EMG changes; our binary q values, tie-free sampling and finite n are independent simplifications.
9. No author R scripts executed, no source input datasets, no real people, no original observed cultural choices, no independent historical forecasts. Evidence E1 only.
