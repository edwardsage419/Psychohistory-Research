## B. Mandatory sharp bounds for the **hypothetical all-16-human slots** cooperation estimand
For original network i, round t, define observed human C count c_it, observed human action n_it (between 1 and 16), unknown m_it=16-n_it. Observed-only rate is r_it=c_it/n_it. If every unobserved human slot were assigned a hypothetical binary C/D response, the sharp feasible whole-roster mean is L_it=c_it/16 and U_it=(c_it+m_it)/16, under no constraints on missingness mechanism. First-to-last per-network change lower dL_i=L_i,30-U_i,1, upper dU_i=U_i,30-L_i,1. Average **equally across all eight original networks per arm**, and equally across all 64 networks for the overall bound. Report both limits, R080 observed-only mean delta for comparison, end-point observed coverage and missing human-slot totals, and number of networks individually forced to increase or decrease for all completions. Allow arbitrary missing action assignments separately across rounds; this is an identification region, not an imputation, and no statistical confidence interval.

The only predeclared cross-arm descriptive change contrasts are `disengaged_mgmt_bots minus no_bots`, `engaged_mgmt_bots minus no_bots`, and `single_mgmt_bot minus no_bots`. Contrast lower=first-arm lower minus second-arm upper; contrast upper=first-arm upper minus second-arm lower. The third contrast joins two different experiment phases and is explicitly not a treatment causal effect. Report any sign reversals and all negative cases; no fitted regression, tests, p-values, or selective arm dropping.
