## Prespecified artificial generative model
Hidden state S in {0,1}, prior b=P(S=1) in {1/4,1/2,3/4}. Two policies: PRECISE sensor with P(O=S|S,PRECISE)=3/4, and NOISY sensor with P(O=S|S,NOISY)=1/2. Preferences C(O=1)=c in {1/2,3/4}; C(O=0)=1-c. PRECISE has exogenous cost k in {0,1/8,1/4}; NOISY cost zero. Thus 3*2*3=18 worlds, 2 policies, 2 hidden states, 2 outcomes, exactly 144 joint source rows. All joint probabilities are rational and strictly positive. No real participants, neural recordings, or historical observations.

## Frozen computations and falsifiers
For each policy a, predicted q_a(o)=sum_s q(s)P(o|s,a), epistemic I_a=sum_{s,o}q(s,o|a) ln[P(o|s,a)/q_a(o)], pragmatic L_a=sum_o q_a(o) ln C(o). Score G_a=-L_a-I_a+cost(a). Independent equivalent score G_a=KL(q_a(o)||C(o))+sum_s q(s)H[P(o|s,a)]+cost(a). Select the lower G, with NOISY on exact ties. The two expressions must agree within 1e-12. All natural logarithms. Every source row retains b,c,k,a,s,o, joint mass and observation mass, plus world policy scores and selected action. CSV and JSON must be deterministic byte-replay outputs.

Predictions fixed before data: for b=1/2,c=1/2,k=0, PRECISE wins because it has positive information gain while NOISY has zero. For b=1/2,c=1/2,k=1/4, NOISY wins because PRECISE information gain is less than 1/4. For every world NOISY epistemic information equals zero (tolerance 1e-12). For every world G direct equals G risk+ambiguity. Any failure rejects E1. Independent auditor cannot import production code, must rederive joint masses and scores and reject deliberate tampering of one row.
