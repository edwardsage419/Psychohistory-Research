## Frozen full enumeration and exact one-step probabilistic law

Fixed **N=5 distinct hypothetical agents**, only compartment counts observed. Enumerate every nonnegative count triple (S,I,R) satisfying S+I+R=5: **21 complete artificial initial states**. Per-contact transmission parameter p∈{0,1/2,1}, old-infective recovery parameter q∈{0,1/2,1}. This yields **21×3×3=189 frozen initial model worlds**. In one discrete step, EACH initially susceptible receives independent Bernoulli potential infection contacts from each of I initially infectious, one probability p for each potential ordered infectious→susceptible pair; if any contact succeeds, susceptible converts to new infectious, never to recovered within the same step. In parallel, each of the I *initially* infected independently recovers with probability q. Originally recovered remain recovered forever. All contacts and recoveries conditionally independent. No contacts from newly infected within the same step, and newly infected cannot recover in this same step. Thus conditional probability newly infected susceptible is a=1−(1−p)^I. Conditional random X∼Binomial(S,a), Y∼Binomial(I,q), mutually independent. Next (S',I',R')=(S−X, I+X−Y, R+Y). Note q=1 removes all OLD infectives but not new infections.

The COMPLETE SUPPORT is enumerated with x∈0..S and y∈0..I for every initial world, **including zero-probability** events for p/q boundaries. Source per-row weight w=comb(S,x)*a^x*(1−a)^(S−x)*comb(I,y)*q^y*(1−q)^(I−y). 21 states and 9 p/q values give **1134 fully labeled original artificial source rows**:
