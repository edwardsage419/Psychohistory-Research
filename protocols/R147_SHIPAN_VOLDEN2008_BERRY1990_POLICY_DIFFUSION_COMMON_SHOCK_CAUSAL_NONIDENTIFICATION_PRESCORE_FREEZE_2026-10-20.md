## Experiment 1: frozen observational equivalence and interventions
Three jurisdictions A (initial leader), B and C (followers). At t=0, U ~ Bernoulli(1/2). At t=1, two follower draws B,C are conditionally independent given each model's parent and have P(adopt|parent=1)=3/4 and P(adopt|parent=0)=1/4, with all draws independent conditional on the parent. 

Mechanism D (direct policy diffusion): A := U; B,C respond to A. Under do(A=a) surgically set A=a but U still random; P(B=1|do(A=1))=3/4, P(B=1|do(A=0))=1/4; causal risk difference 1/2.
Mechanism S (shared exogenous shock): U ~ Bern(1/2); A:=U; B,C respond to U, NOT A. Same complete observed joint P(A,B,C) for all 8 possible adoption histories, yet P(B=1|do(A=a))=1/2 for either a; causal risk difference zero. Observational conditional risk difference P(B=1|A=1)-P(B=1|A=0)=1/2 in BOTH worlds. This *perfectly collinear* hidden-U toy is a valid non-identification witness; do not claim population-wide identification without positivity of all U/A conditional strata. Also test P(B=C) exact; B,C are correlated unconditionally and independent conditionally given parent. No physical geography is measured.
Compute all 8 joint mass fractions independently by (i) full Bernoulli elementary worlds and (ii) closed-form factorizations. Assert all positive joint masses, total=1; use an actual do-operation on the structural generator, not conditioning on A after seeing data. Show that observational association cannot, without extra assumptions, prove peer causation.

## Experiment 2: EXACT time-ordered adoption hazard on 4 jurisdictions
Undirected graphs: path edges 0–1,1–2,2–3; star center0 edges 0–1,0–2,0–3; ring edges 0–1,1–2,2–3,3–0. Start at t=0 with ONLY jurisdiction 0 having adopted (policy cannot be repealed). Each of four later discrete steps: draw shared annual shock Z_t independently Bernoulli(s), s in {0,1/2}, same Z_t for ALL nodes in that year. Each non-adopter i independently flips with 
p_i(t|historical adopted state,Z_t) = 1/8 + beta * (number of adopted neighbors at PRIOR step / number of neighbors) + (1/4)*Z_t,
with beta ∈{0,1/2}. Max p <= 7/8, so no truncation; zero-degree treated exposure=0 (not needed in three selected graphs). Existing adopters stay adopted. Each yearly move uses the SAME prior state, so no within-year cascade; draw conditional independence only given Z_t. Frozen 3*2*2=12 graph,peer,shock cases. For each output exact distribution over all 16 final adoption-bitmask states (probabilities Fraction); expected final adopters, expected new adopters, probability all 4 adopted, and P(a given node adopted) as needed. Independently implement:
 - path A: Markov state-mass DP with conditionally independent shock mix, explicit enumeration next state;
 - path B: brute exhaustive edge transition events using independent local Bernoulli draw recursion for each state, shock and period; no reuse of A transition implementation.
For beta=0, graph choice must not matter when shocks fixed; path/star/ring final distributions identical, and for s>0 adoption states are marginally correlated even when beta=0 due to shared shocks. For beta>0, check monotone expected adoption in beta under same graph, same shocks via exact calculation, but do not assert a graph ordering across all metrics without result. Check probability-mass conservation in each period. Comparing beta models is causal because parameters are assigned by construction, not because observed histories establish policy influence. No fit to original US city data.
Output separate source-use and identification-limit fields. No stochastic Monte Carlo or simulated human 'samples' are to be called empirical.
