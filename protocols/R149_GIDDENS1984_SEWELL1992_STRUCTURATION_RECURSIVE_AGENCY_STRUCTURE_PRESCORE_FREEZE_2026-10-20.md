## Frozen project-built finite recursion
Two synthetic actors i=0,1. Structural support S_t in {0,1,2,3,4}; S is rule/resources affordance indicator **not** Giddens' empirical measurement. Privileged material resource endowment actor0 r∈{0,1}; actor1 always 1. Common period opportunity signal Z_t Bernoulli(q), q∈{0,1/2}, independent across periods. Given the same prior S_t and current Z_t, actors choose binary enabling practice A_i,t ∈{0,1}, independent conditional on (S,Z):
P(A_i,t=1 | S,Z)=1/8 + S/8 + r_i/8 + Z/8, with r_0=r, r_1=1. All probabilities in [1/8,7/8], no clamping and no individual optimization/strategic best response. There is an *exactly specified* resource asymmetry with r0=0 vs 1.
For feedback mode f=1, define S_{t+1}=min(4,max(0,S_t + sign(A_0,t + A_1,t − 1))) where A total2 ⇒ +1; total1 ⇒ 0; total0 ⇒ −1. For frozen-structure negative control f=0, S_{t+1}=S_t regardless of actions. Initial S0∈{0,2,4}; horizon T=6. Actors choose simultaneously with respect to PRIOR structure; no within-period order effect. No discount, output metric total practice actions across both actors and all periods, expectation at horizon; terminal state exact probabilities and expected terminal S. This is a custom synthetic Markov example of recursion/structuration only. Exogenous common opportunity may correlate actions without endogenous structure, and resource endowment may change rates without causal policy intervention.
24 frozen scenarios = f∈{0,1} × q∈{0,1/2} × r∈{0,1} × S0∈{0,2,4}. Full PMF of 5 states for **every 0..6 period**, expected practice actions each period and cumulated expected practice actions, P(S6=4), and probability of regime shift S6≠S0.
