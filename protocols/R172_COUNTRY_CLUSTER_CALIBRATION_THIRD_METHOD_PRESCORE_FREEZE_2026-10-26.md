## Frozen scope and computations
1. Parse all rows of the existing heldout prediction CSV without filtering. Require exactly 319 rows, 35 unique episode IDs, 32 unique country IDs, 18 positive annual outcomes, 2000 through 2017 calendar years, valid probabilities strictly between 0 and 1, and one observed event at most per episode. Report violations as FAIL. Preserve original M0/M1/M2 exact fraction strings; no fitting, refitting, threshold tuning or prediction editing.
2. For each M0, M1 and M2 calculate mean annual negative Bernoulli log likelihood and mean Brier squared error. Match published 2026-10-21 R172 report within absolute tolerance 1e-10. Report total expected event count sum(p), observed count, expected/observed ratio, and fixed-bin observed-versus-expected calibration. Fixed disjoint bins: [0,0.02), [0.02,0.05), [0.05,1]. Empty bins remain explicitly empty.
3. Compare M1-M0 and M2-M0 on the same 319 annual rows. The inferential unit is country ID, because different episodes may share a country. Enumerate all leave-one-country-out (LOCO) estimates of weighted annual mean loss differences, using the remaining rows as denominator; report minimum, maximum, number of positive/negative differences, and which country deletions change the sign relative to full-data difference. Do not interpret LOCO as a new independent test set.
4. Perform 1024 country-cluster bootstrap draws with replacement, sample exactly K distinct-country labels per draw (K=number of unique countries), and include all rows of each sampled country with multiplicity. Fixed seed 20261026 and PRNG: state=(1664525*state+1013904223) mod 2^32; draw index=floor(K*state/2^32). Countries sorted lexicographically; 1024 replicates. Report 2.5% and 97.5% quantiles by sorted linear interpolation at (B-1)*q for annual mean logloss and Brier differences M1-M0 and M2-M0. Compare with prior episode-level 512-bootstrap results but do not pool intervals or claim confidence coverage under arbitrary country dependence.
5. Predeclared negative controls: flip the first row's binary event and recompute its M0 logloss, which must change; replace first row's M2 probability with 1/2 and recompute its Brier, which must change. Both controls are executed on disposable copies only.
6. Record input blob, code identity, exact counts, full country-level influence table, bootstrap quantiles and a machine-readable receipt. Report all results, including null, sign changes and worse baselines. No model selection or score adjustment after observing results.
